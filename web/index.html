The catalog's eo:cloud_cover describes a 110 km × 110 km scene. CHIP measures it over your actual AOI using the SCL band. The gap between these two columns is the whole point.   red gap = catalog understates (scene was cloudier than stated over your AOI)  |  green gap = catalog overstates (image was usable but would have been filtered out).
